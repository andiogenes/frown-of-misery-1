Controls

KeysPlay: 4 voices sharing one shape, each with its own pitch, or MONO (legato). Notes also go out over USB-MIDI.
ALGORITHMThe shape: 48 of them, from CSAW to QUESTION MARK
EDITThe next page of parameters. KNOB 1–4 turn the four on the screen.
Page OSCTIMBRE, COLOR (what they do depends on the shape, and the screen says), FINE tune, LEVEL
Page AMPATTACK, RELEASE (0 ms to 24 s), GLIDE, VOICES (POLY 4 / MONO)
SELECTThe engine: BRAIDS or RINGS
FXClouds after the engine, or not; the boxes under the parameters show the route. MIDI program change 1–4: BRAIDS, RINGS, BRAIDS → CLOUDS, RINGS → CLOUDS.
RECClouds' FREEZE: the buffer stops recording, the grains keep playing what it holds
Rings, ALGORITHMThe model: MODAL RESONATOR, SYMPATHETIC STRINGS or STRING
Rings, page RESSTRUCTURE, BRIGHTNESS, DAMPING, POSITION: Rings' four knobs. STRUCTURE tunes the sympathetic strings and sets the string's dispersion.
Rings, page PARTPOLY (1, 2 or 4 voices taking the strikes in turn), FINE tune, LEVEL. No note-off: a key strikes, DAMPING sets how long it rings.
Clouds, page GRAINPOSITION, SIZE, DENSITY, TEXTURE: Clouds' granular mode (DENSITY: regular grains left of noon, random right, none at noon)
Clouds, page MIXBLEND (dry/wet), SPREAD, FEEDBACK, REVERB
Clouds, page PITCHPITCH, −24 … +24 semitones; QUALITY HI or LO (Clouds' low fidelity: 8-bit, 16 kHz, less CPU; always LO after the sympathetic strings)
OCT- / OCT+Octave
PRESETSMIDI channel: OMNI, or 1 to 16
MIDI inNotes with velocity, pitch bend ±2 semitones, clock display (USB and TRS)

Install

Install from the browser: Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser, over USB. Or from a terminal, with the source checked out:

python3 tools/fm1_install.py frown.fwsc

Or use the FM-1 Firmware Hub, which also takes you back to any other firmware. If a build does not start, hold OCT- while switching on to reach the USB rescue. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

FROWN OF MISERY

Mutable Instruments' Braids, and later Rings and Clouds, as a firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 (and the SMK-37).

An early development build from the newest commit, not a release. It has not been played on a real FM-1 yet. The emulator runs the same package you would install.

Play it here

Press Start, then play with the computer keyboard, the mouse, a touch screen or a MIDI keyboard. Full page · SMK-37

Controls

KeysPlay, 4 voices sharing one shape, each with its own pitch. Notes also go out over USB-MIDI.
ALGORITHMThe shape: 48 of them, from CSAW to QUESTION MARK
KNOB 1 / 2TIMBRE / COLOR. What they do depends on the shape, and the screen says.
KNOB 3 / 4Attack / release, 0 ms to 24 s
OCT- / OCT+Octave
PRESETSMIDI channel: OMNI, or 1 to 16
MIDI inNotes with velocity, pitch bend ±2 semitones, clock display (USB and TRS)

Install

python3 tools/fm1_install.py frown.fwsc

Or use the FM-1 Firmware Hub, which also takes you back to any other firmware. If a build does not start, hold OCT- while switching on to reach the USB rescue. Installing firmware is at your own risk.
